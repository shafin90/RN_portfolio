Expertise

Same hard problems, different products.

Search · Booking · Payments · Authentication · Dashboards · Notification System

Search

Finding is per type, not one box. Programs by title and place, stories by name and healing, donations by name and transaction — each narrowed at query by status, featured, branch, currency, and date. Donations keep a dedicated transaction lookup beside general search, with export under the same filters.

Elsewhere the same care with different constraints. Buses and stations stay inside their own house so a new city never disturbs old ones. Campaigns check eligibility and budget before showing, with noisy place names cleaned once at entry. Musicians order by presence then nearness, with intent as a hard gate. The user finds in one try because the query asked precisely.

Booking

My rule never changes: a hold ends by itself, and ticket and money move together. Boats hold briefly and return quietly when payment misses. Buses hold per house with expiry sweeping the abandoned and seats releasing on failure. A repeated press finds its first result. Offline sales wait their turn and rejoin per house with the server deciding clashes.

The same lifecycle guards money elsewhere. Donations pressed three times count once with one receipt. Surveys pay once from the right campaign and balance. Matching ships behind flags so unfinished paths never scale early. No oversell at the gate, no stuck seats, no midnight cleanup for owners.

Payments

I verify on the server and never trust the client callback. Donations confirm provider-side with webhook-driven status, rates frozen at initiation, receipts uploaded where a human must verify, confirmations and transfer instructions mailed. Boat orders recount the bill server-side and refuse mismatch. Guests check out by phone with no account needed.

Providers change down the same guarded path — card, mobile money, top-ups and payouts — with type checks, request limits, and webhook confirmation before balances move. Refunds know their method with partial accounting, audit, and notice, and exhausted paths surface to ops instead of vanishing. Every payment resolves to one truth finance can close on.

Authentication

Identity fits the user, not the template. Where phones are the identity, the code received at sign-up stays permanent — returners type it with no message, the forgotten receive a fresh one that replaces it, attempts count toward lockout, and tokens never ride back in responses. Where OAuth fits, sign-in flows through one path with code recovery reusing the same session.

Roles stay narrow everywhere — who may publish, edit, refund, approve, or read — with revocation that actually revokes and per-house isolation so one company never sees another's trips, money, or messages. Registration creates user, profile, chat identity, and preferences together, so nothing orphans and nothing races.

Dashboards

Each house sees only its truth and can act on it. Bookings, trips, fleet, crew, routes, stops, users, content, and theme — scoped per company with role-based routing, live trips, and per-house counts for bookings, revenue, and occupancy. Verification queues and report filters narrow by state so ops clears work instead of exporting mysteries.

Smaller operations get the same honesty scaled down. Program stats with branch and date filters, donation export under active filters with currency lists and status across pending to refunded. Boat occupancy and revenue with today's view and payment signals. Campaigns with users, transactions, and notices side by side. A founder reads the business in one screen, and staff act without calling me.

Notification System

I assume no signal and a dead app, then build up. Prayer reminders rebuild from programs and schedules on every launch, in each program's language, with quiet permission first — and fire with no server. Trip notices arrive localized over personal channels with push config traveling with deploys. Tokens register on launch and again at verification, alerts present in foreground and persist in background.

Every notice opens its exact screen — the booking, the thread, the payout, the match — with back intact. Notices name only what changed. Where push cannot reach, the message falls back so the user still learns. Follow pings its person, refunds explain themselves, departures warn in time. The message arrives after the system kills the app, and that is what brings people back.

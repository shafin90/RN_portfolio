Common questions

Things founders ask me.

Native or hybrid — what do you recommend?

Whichever fits the problem. Hybrid when speed and a shared codebase matter. Native when you need maximum performance or deep device integration. I'll tell you honestly what I'd choose and why.

We have a web app — can you reuse anything?

Yes. I typically share 40% of code between web and mobile — business logic, data models, and API layers. Your existing codebase becomes a head start.

Our users have spotty internet.

I've shipped three production apps that work offline. Content is stored on-device and syncs when connection returns. Jet Express runs across 10 African countries. SRKAN works with zero signal.

Will you think about architecture, or just build screens?

Both. Every project gets clean architecture, proper state management, and monitoring from day one. Your next hire will understand the codebase.

What happens after launch?

Crash monitoring, analytics, and the ability to push updates without waiting for app store review — all set up before we launch. I stay available for iteration.

Do you only take on new builds?

No. I also join existing teams — fixing performance issues, stabilizing crashes, or owning the mobile side alongside your engineers.

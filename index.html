Featured case study

Legato — where I learned to hold a product calm

Legato is where I learned to hold a product calm — musician profiles, discovery, follow, block, groups, chat, push. Small US team, both stores. My part was the phone as it is, and the server agreeing with it.

I work failure first. A wrong order means a wrong partner. A follow with no notice kills the loop. A tap at root kills the reply. So search stays honest — one query ranked, faces first then distance, no second sort on the phone. Matching ships to Explore behind a flag — scored apart, precomputed, reads cheap. Follow notifies without risking the follow. Taps open that exact thread, back intact.

State writes moved into actions. Lists unified with guards. Releases kept shippable through major upgrades with crash reporting from the start. Ordered, scored, notified, back intact — every failure owned before it reached a musician.

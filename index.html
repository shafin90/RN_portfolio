Featured case study

Legato — where I learned to hold a product calm

Legato, a musician collaboration platform — profiles, discovery, follow, block, groups, chat, push. Small US team, both stores. My part was the phone as it is, and the server agreeing with it.

I kept search honest — one query ranked, faces first then distance, no second sort on the phone. I shipped matching to Explore behind a flag — scored apart from search, precomputed, reads cheap, with viewed, dismissed, and refresh paths. Follow notifies without risking the follow. Block hides everywhere. Taps open that exact thread, back intact.

State writes moved into actions. Lists unified with guards. Releases kept shippable through major upgrades with crash reporting from the start. The product held — ordered, scored, notified, back intact.

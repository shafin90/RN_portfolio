Featured case study

Shipping Legato in 9 months

Legato, a musician collaboration platform, needed iOS and Android apps live in 9 months for an investor demo: sign-in with Google and Apple, profiles with skills, discovery by name, skill, and proximity, and collaboration flows wired into chat and push notifications.

I own both sides — the Expo client and the API behind it — so mobile, backend, and dashboard never disagree.

The risk was the demo itself — one crash or one broken rollout in front of investors. I kept releases safe with Expo SDK upgrades, EAS staged rollouts, and feature flags, so unfinished collaboration paths never reached users before they proved out. Identity went through Better Auth on a custom Postgres adapter, with registration provisioning the profile, Stream Chat identity, and initial skills in one pass. Notifications went dual-channel, GetStream plus Firebase, with badge counts synced and taps deep-linking to the exact chat.

Result: live on both stores, zero crashes at demo. The same discipline runs in my other products: SRKAN works with zero signal, Jet Express sells offline across 30+ cities and syncs per tenant, Govihari totals on-device to match the server exactly, and KavooGo's earning loop is idempotent so a retry can never double-pay.

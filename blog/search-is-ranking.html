Search is ranking, not a text box

Search fails in three places and only three: nothing matches that should, the wrong things rank first, or the right things arrive too late. Every search project I have worked on reduced, after the first month of complaints, to one of those three sentences. The discipline is to find out which sentence you are living in before writing code, because each one points at a different layer — matching and normalization, ranking and query shape, indexes and payload size. Teams that skip the diagnosis rebuild the text box. Teams that diagnose fix the layer and watch the complaints stop.
